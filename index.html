About Us

A moving service that treats your home like its own.

Sivamayam is a South India–focused packing and moving service, built around four dedicated lorries and a crew that plans every route with care. From a single almirah to a full household, we pack, load, secure and deliver — with a hand-tied tarpaulin cover and a driver who knows the roads.

We work across Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, for local shifts within a city and long interstate moves alike. No middlemen, no surprise charges — just a straightforward quote and a truck that shows up on time.
